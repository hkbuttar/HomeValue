# Neighborhood price indices

This analysis builds three complementary annual neighborhood series:

- median PPSF index, retained as a transparent transaction-mix measure;
- property-adjusted index based on median residual log price after a pooled
  property-only hedonic model;
- neighborhood repeat-sales index where at least five consistent-geography
  repeat pairs support estimation.

Each series is normalized to 100 in its neighborhood's first retained year.
Neighborhood-years with fewer than five sales are omitted from the main panel.
The property-adjusted model deliberately excludes time and neighborhood controls
so their combined market signal remains in the residual index.

Co-movement is measured as pairwise correlation in annual log changes, requiring
at least three overlapping growth years. Divergence ranks each neighborhood's
latest cumulative change from its own base. These results are descriptive and
remain sensitive to transaction mix, sparse years, renovations, and repeat-sale
selection.
